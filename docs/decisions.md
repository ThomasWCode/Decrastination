# Decisions

The owner's decisions that govern how the app is developed, numbered so code and docs can point at them ("(D-21)"). Each gives the decision, what it means for the code, and where it applies; dates are 2026. Only the latest form of a decision is kept; "(not: …)" names an older form where it might be proposed again.

How the app works in detail is in [spec/](spec/); how the work is done is in [development.md](development.md). A new decision gets the next number in the PR that applies it; one that changes is edited in place, with its new date.

## Principles

- **D-1. An organiser first, a blocker second** (2.0.0, 12 Oct). The work (deadlines, minutes, days, steps) can always be changed at once; only switching blocking off is guarded (D-46).
- **D-2. The core stays deterministic.** Ranking, deadlines, allocation, quotas and "done" never depend on Claude, whose readings are advice the rules check and can stand in for. Applies: `core/`.
- **D-3. One phone, everything on it.** No PC or server at runtime; a sideloaded app for its one owner.
- **D-4. The app's rules apply to the app's own output; what you or Felix set explicitly is kept as set** (17 Oct). A limit or adjustment the app makes (sittings, the learned pace) never rewrites your or Felix's values; where the app's knowledge would help with them, Felix is told it instead. Applies: every enforcement or normalisation, with `TaskItem.mine` (your and Felix's values, each with who set it), `yourSteps` and `yourMinutes` exempt.
- **D-5. Quality over cost.** Claude is tuned for good readings and speed, not cheapness; but no call is wasted (D-90, D-91).

## Sources

The widget app must stay installed: it's the Teams reader; its home-screen widget itself is optional. Merging its backend into this one isn't worth it now; it's worth revisiting only if changes across both repositories become frequent. Changes to it follow its own repository; this app reads a new column only when it's there. Applies: `sources/teams/`.
- **D-7. Sources are only ever read, but for one write** (amended 21 Oct, 4.3.0, Q19: A): finishing a starred email's work here unstars and archives it in Gmail, behind a switch (D-106). Otherwise the only thing the app writes outside itself is its own events in the calendar you chose.
- **D-8. Power Planner through its web API**, credentials encrypted, polled every 15 minutes (its rate limit accepted); its timetable is used, free periods being classes named "Study Period". Applies: `sources/powerplanner/`.
- **D-9. AnkiDroid through its provider.** Homework is matched to decks by textbook section, with a current-textbook setting (Q18). The quota: every due review, 20 new cards from the current textbook's lowest deck, and decks homework names, due 21:30; on a day homework vocabulary is due, reviews only; counted in cards. Applies: `sources/anki/`.
- **D-10. Gmail: only starred conversations, each a task** (3.0.1). IMAP with an app password; every starred conversation is something to do (an event's email included), read whole with its replies; unstarring finishes it (DX-03), or its work finished here, which unstars it (D-106), and, as every starred email is Admin, that earns free time as any finished task does (17 Oct). (Not: the whole inbox, or only emails that ask for something.) Applies: `sources/gmail/`, `core/Merge.kt`.
- **D-11. Teams syncs** on a tap, on the first unlock after the evening start, and every 3 hours; each automatic one shows a 10-second banner with Cancel and Delay, only while the phone's in use and never in quiet hours (Q21). Asking Felix to check Teams with the chat on screen is a tap too (Q33: B, 20 Oct): the widget syncs then; from his notification's reply, or with the chat left, he reads what the widget has and says so.
- **D-12. The same work from two sources is linked by a tap, never automatically** (DX-15).
- **D-13. Other wellbeing apps on the phone are left alone.**
- **D-106. A starred email finished here is unstarred and archived in Gmail** (4.3.0, 21 Oct; docs/plan_v4/10-emails-done.md, Q18: A but its failure the app's own notification, Q19: A, Q27: A): its last piece kept or last step ticked, Felix's `done`, or the sheet's *Finish* journals the write, which goes a minute later; an Undo within that minute leaves Gmail untouched, after it a write puts back only what was taken. The very first waits for your OK (*Carry on*, *Not this time*, *Turn this off*). Every starred message of the conversation is unstarred and the conversation archived: nothing deleted, moved or marked read. Gmail's website still draws a star whose kind it keeps in a label IMAP can't take off, till it's clicked off there: Gmail's limit, accepted (21 Oct evening; spec/sources.md). The task closes at the Gmail read after it, as one unstarred by hand does (D-10), earning as any finished task does. A write that fails five times is given up and said by the app's own notification (*Your sources*), with what did and didn't happen. Settings → Sources → Gmail turns it off; Felix, asked then to finish an email, says he can't finish it in Gmail and asks whether to mark it not a task instead, never calling it done (21 Oct evening, 4.3.1). Applies: `core/GmailFinish.kt`, `core/GmailWrites.kt`, `sync/GmailFinishes.kt`, `sync/GmailWriteWorker.kt`, `sources/gmail/ImapWriter.kt`.

## Claude's readings

- **D-14. Claude is opt-in; the rules stand in when it's off.** Applies: `RuleEnricher` (`enrich/Enricher.kt`).
- **D-15. One model for every job**, thinking hard on the readings the plan is made from and less where someone waits (AC-02).
- **D-16. A task is read once, and again only when its content changes** (`Enrichments.inputHash`). A change to the prompts (`ReadingPrompts.VERSION`) reads open tasks again once, except those with a step of their reading done (AC-06).
- **D-17. A reading that fails a check is put back once, then salvaged, then dropped** (AI-01).
- **D-18. Only a real deadline can be missed:** a source's, one you gave, or an email's own words as Claude quotes them. Every date the app or Claude sets is a plan, never overdue (PL-14, AI-12).
- **D-19. A reading the text can't settle asks one question, through Felix** (AI-10). Your reply is kept as its answer only where it answers it: one that doesn't (a question of yours, a remark, another task) is answered in the chat, and his question waits again; an answer only tells, so it changes nothing it merely says of the work (4.3.12, 23 Oct, your call when a question of yours, replied to his, was kept as the answer).
- **D-20. Steps are as few as the work has** (3.1.1, 17 Oct): one step unless the instructions list separate pieces of work; dated blocks only for separately dated pieces; a few minutes' admin goes in the title of the step it belongs to, never a step of its own.
- **D-21. Nothing is split unless it's over an hour, then by time, never by content** (3.1.2, 17 Oct). Even sittings of an hour at most: the first the work, then "Continue: …", the last "Continue and finish: …"; at reading and again at planning, where the plan's minutes are longer. The app's own steps only (D-34). Applies: `core/Sittings.kt`, `ReadingAnswers.fitParts`, `Planner.sittings`.
- **D-22. Micro-plans for every slot; photo checks take several pages, with no partial credit; a photo can make a task or re-estimate one.**
- **D-23. The profile tells Claude everything useful about you** (your year, subjects, week, Felix's memories) without crowding the context.
- **D-24. The API's model fallback stays on**, and a reading is credited to the model that answered.

## Planner

- **D-25. Work not due today or tomorrow is spread evenly to its deadline** by each day's free time, a piece at least once a week, with no daily maximum (PL-01). (Not: placed backwards from the deadline.)
- **D-26. Work due today or tomorrow comes before overdue work.**
- **D-27. A day's capacity is its free time times the share of it you really use**, learned per kind of day (between 0.5 and 1.0; 1.0 until seven such days) (PL-06). A weekday off school is a weekend's day, planned with the weekend's share and teaching it (17 Oct). Applies: `Planner.capacityOf`, `learn/Days.record`.
- **D-28. A weekend keeps one long window**; two weekend windows aren't built.
- **D-29. Revision goes a day apart**; there's no evening-start preference.
- **D-30. Work can be moved as often as you like, never past its due date**; overdue work can go to any day or time from now, and is planned there, still marked overdue (17 Oct). Applies: `InstructionRules.latePin`, `Planner.keptDay`, `pinnedTime`, `pinProblem`.
- **D-31. A deadline later the same day can use that day's school windows before it** (Q-52).
- **D-32. The planner stays pure**: everything it uses comes in through `Planner.Input`.
- **D-33. A piece's length (`boxMin`) is yours** (17 Oct): 60 minutes on your phone; the weekly review never changes it.
- **D-34. Steps you or Felix gave are planned exactly as given** (17 Oct): never cut into sittings, never stretched by the learned pace (`TaskItem.yourSteps`).
- **D-35. The learned pace stretches only the app's own estimates** (2.0.0; 17 Oct): your minutes, your own tasks and steps you or Felix gave are planned as given (`Calibration.multiplierFor`).
- **D-36. What isn't planned is listed with why** (*Not planned*, on Work, below the tasks); there's no forecasting of work not yet open.
- **D-37. Days off and half-days are told to Felix** (or set in Settings): a day off takes the weekend's rules, a half-day's evening starts at the early finish.
- **D-38. No exams page, recurring work or streaks.**

## Slots and the calendar

- **D-39. Every piece gets a time** in the day, written to the calendar a week ahead and kept up to date as the plan changes (PL-12, NF-07).
- **D-40. Breaks:** five minutes between pieces, a quarter of an hour after an hour and a half of work. School windows are the Study Periods, break and lunch (less time to eat), at least 15 minutes. Each step says where it can be done (anywhere, or home), and you can change it (PL-15).
- **D-41. Weekends and days off get three 20-minute breaks outside**, given up in number (the midday one first) but never shortened; none starts after 18:00, one with no room by then left out (your call, 19 Oct, 4.0.13).
- **D-42. Slots go to a calendar you made in Google Calendar** (one the app makes itself wouldn't sync). A slot you move there pins its piece at your time; an event you add takes the time from a slot; a slot's event you delete comes back only if its time is still free.
- **D-43. The app never deletes an event of yours that it can tell from its own** (3.1.1–3.1.2, 17 Oct). A copy the calendar's sync makes of one of its events is deleted only when it matches a version the app itself wrote earlier (fingerprints in `calendar.json`); an event already there under a new slot's exact title, times and wording is never taken for one. The limit: one you make later as an exact copy of an earlier slot can't be told from a sync's copy. Applies: `calendar/SlotCalendarDiff.kt`.
- **D-44. Calendar events count as busy time, judged one by one**: the app asks when it can't tell, and one answer covers every event of that name (Q7). Until you answer, an event asked about takes all its time (17 Oct). Applies: `learn/EventJudge.kt`.
- **D-108. A task's steps keep their order within a day too** (4.3.4, 22 Oct, Q56: A): a step not yet begun goes no earlier than the end of its task's steps before it, and waits unslotted while one of them has no time that day, even where an earlier gap would hold it; a gap only a later step fits goes to other work. A step you pinned stays where you put it (D-4), and one under way stays where it is. Found at that morning's check: a short revising step had gone to lunch, before the planning, the research and the draft it follows, and keeping its slot would have ticked it. The cost, accepted: a gap at school takes only a task's next step, so more of a task's steps go to the evening, and a step with no time leaves those after it untimed that day (still the day's work). Across days the plan takes a day's untimed work as done that day, so a later step can be forecast for the next day ahead of it until that day's own plan lays them in order (23 Oct, Q61: A). Next, each a PR of its own (the plan's rows): Felix marks the steps that don't depend on the one before, which can then fill a gap as before (D-109, 4.3.5); then, in a school day's 07:00 briefing call, he may rebalance two adjacent steps of his own not yet begun so the next fits a gap (D-110, 4.3.6) (still two steps, their minutes adding up as before, neither under 15), shown with Undo (22 Oct, Q57, Q58). Applies: `core/Slotter.kt`.
- **D-109. A step can say it doesn't need the steps before it** (4.3.5, 22 Oct, Q57): the reading marks it (`anyOrder`) on an assignment's steps and on an email's or planner item's blocks, and Felix on the steps he gives (a Steps change, a new task's), where the step builds on nothing before it (separate questions or exercises, a reading task beside vocabulary to learn, separate applications); the first step's is false, having none before it. Such a step, in one piece, may go into a day's gap ahead of the steps before it, D-108's order binding only the steps that need theirs, and a step after it still follows it; one in sittings follows them, as a sitting kept is counted on the task's steps in their order, and of a block the reading cuts into parts only the first may be free. A step you give needs those before it, as you gave it, unless your words to Felix say otherwise or you mark it on its row in the form, which shows each step's mark but the first's as an "Any order" chip to set or clear (22 Oct, Q60: A, 4.3.7). The task sheet says "in any order" beside it, and Felix sees it in the chat's list of steps. Applies: `core/Slotter.kt`, `SubStep.anyOrder` and `OwnStep.anyOrder`, `enrich/ReadingPrompts.kt` (version 9), `enrich/InstructionPrompts.kt`, `felix/ChatPrompts.kt`.
- **D-110. Two of Felix's or the reading's steps can be rebalanced to fit a free time at school** (4.3.6, 22 Oct, Q58): in a school day's 07:00 briefing call, no call of its own, Felix is shown each task whose next step, today's work, his own or the reading's (4.3.8, 22 Oct, your call: most work that needs it comes from Teams and email), not begun and one that can be done at school, is too long for a free time at school before its slot today, with the step after it that needs it, alike in place, with dates that plan the work moved to it no later than the first's did: none later than a date of the first's own still to come, and open by now or with the first (a step's own date gone by is a plan's, dropped, PL-14), so steps with dates of their own, as he gives a big task's, are rebalanced once the first's date has gone by, or where the two share it (22 Oct, after your note); the step laid at its minutes as planned, the reading's by the pace learned (not shared out to a total you gave, nor shortened by work or its source's progress), and each task given a free time of its own that its piece can be done in. Where the work is made of separate parts (questions, sources, sections), he moves the boundary between the two so the first fits that time: still two steps, with their ids, their minutes adding up as before, the first no longer than the time, neither under 15; never cut by time (the first half of a video). Checked again once the call is back (a task finished or begun meanwhile isn't changed), and tried on the plan first, with those made before it, so it's made only where its first step is laid in the free time he was shown, its rests kept (where one he gave can't be made, the morning is the day's list in the app's words, as his would say it was), it's made at once as his Steps change, its card under his message with its Undo, and the starts kept today for the steps it changes let go of, so they're laid afresh, the task's others keeping theirs, so his message's times for them hold (4.3.10, 23 Oct: at 4.3.6's check the task's later steps moved from the times he'd named); a task whose rebalance is taken back isn't asked about again while the chat holds it. A task of the reading's with a step still to do over an hour as planned isn't asked about, as his steps are never cut into sittings (D-34), nor one with a step an Anki deck holds, which as his would be planned twice; and two of the reading's only where, however he splits them, the second stays within an hour (their sum less the shortest first, 15 minutes, at most 60). Applies: `core/Rebalance.kt`, `Slotter.schoolGaps`, `felix/Looks.kt`, `felix/FelixPrompts.kt`, `Planning.calibration`.

## Blocking and protection

- **D-45. Blocking is an accessibility service and a full-screen block screen, with no bypass.**
- **D-46. Once armed, only loosening blocking is guarded** (2.0.0, 12 Oct): disarming; taking apps, sites or browsers off the lists; longer quiet hours; a later school-day start; fewer work minutes per free minute (the weekly review's own change aside, D-71); a shorter wait. Those take the 24-hour wait or the override code; everything about the work changes at once (due dates included). (Not: due-date changes needing the code.) Applies: `protect/`.
- **D-47. The override is a code from an authenticator app a parent holds** (Q17); nothing is ever emailed. Protection is friction, not security.
- **D-48. The self-repair permission is granted over adb** (Q19).
- **D-49. What's blocked:** video and social apps, their sites in Chrome and Brave, and Firefox and Tor outright while blocking applies. Never blocked: calls, messages, study apps, maps and travel, banking, authenticators, AI assistants, health and support apps.
- **D-50. When:** from 16:45 on school days and 10:00 at weekends and on days off, never in quiet hours (22:30–07:00) (Q6, Q6b); only today's slots keep it on.
- **D-51. Free time:** about a minute for every three of work, gone at midnight, never banked; blocked apps can't be used for study. It can be spent in the gaps between today's slots; a slot missed today keeps tonight's block on, a move you make lifts it.
- **D-52. A tick keeps any slot**; a piece under 20 minutes earns on its tick, a longer one with a photo (later the same day too), never more a day than its plan (BL-08). Finishing a task earns by the lower of your minutes and the app's estimate at the pace learned, each in its own scale.
- **D-53. A day with nothing planned opens with an hour of free time.**
- **D-54. Done on your own tasks earns nothing; a source's task can't be ticked done by hand**: its source says when it's done (Q28), but a starred email, which finishing here unstars, so Gmail says it (D-106).
- **D-55. Focus sessions are gone and stay gone**: slot outcomes are the record (LR-09).
- **D-56. The blocker is checked by hand on the phone**; there's no emulator harness.
