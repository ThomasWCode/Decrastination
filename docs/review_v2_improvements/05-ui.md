# 5. UI

The screens (`ui/*`), the widget (`widget/*`) and the block screen (`block/BlockedActivity.kt`). The app's UI is plain and consistent, which suits a student who needs to be told one thing; the items here keep that and mostly remove taps or typing. Where another document's improvement lands on a screen (Claude's reason on the sheet, the load line on a day) it's named here but specified there.

## UI-01 Time pickers instead of typed times

**Now.** Hours, quiet hours, the briefing time and the check-in time are typed as text ("16:45") and parsed (`ui/SettingsActivity.kt:347`, `parseTime` at `:365`). A typo is an error message.

**Proposal.** Material 3's `TimePicker` in a dialog from a tap on the field; the field shows the time and stays read-only. The `Window` fields become two taps (from, to) with the window's length shown ("5 h 15").

**Caveats.** None; one component swap. Keep the text parsing for the debug commands.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-02 A load line under each day

**Now.** A day header on the Plan tab is a date (`TodayScreen.DayHeader`, `TodayScreen.kt:419`); the list below it is capped at eight pieces (`PER_DAY`, `:278`) with "and N more".

**Proposal.** Under the date: "2 h 10 planned · 5 h 15 free", and a thin bar in proportion; red past capacity with PL-02's "won't fit" text. Today's line also counts what's done ("1 h 20 done").

**Final state.** The shape of the week is visible without opening anything.

**Caveats.** The capacity is on `DayBucket` already; nothing to compute.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-03 Swipe a piece: start, or tick

**Now.** A piece opens a sheet; Start and the tick are on the sheet or the DO NOW card (`ui/PieceActions.kt`).

**Proposal.** Swipe right on a piece: start a session on it (the DO NOW card's Start). Swipe left: tick the step (own tasks and emails' steps; never a source task's "done", which the source says, Q27). Each with an undo snackbar.

**Caveats.** Accidental swipes in a list that scrolls; Material's `SwipeToDismissBox` needs a threshold and the undo. A tick by swipe is a loosening of sorts (a ticked step earns no free time, so it isn't; only a finished session does).

**Effort.** M. **Decision.** Q-17.

## UI-04 Move a piece to another day by hand

**Now.** "Do it Thursday" goes through Claude (an instruction, one call, a card to tap) or isn't possible.

**Proposal.** Long-press a piece to drag it onto another day's header, writing an `OnDay` change (PL-04) with no model call; and a *Tomorrow* button on the sheet for the common case. The plan re-runs; if the day can't hold it, the sheet says so before the change is kept.

**Caveats.** This makes avoidance one gesture. Limits worth building in: not for a piece due tomorrow or earlier; at most two moves a day; moves logged as instructions so they show in the parent view (NF-02). Whether you want this at all is Q-17.

**Effort.** M. **Decision.** Q-17.

## UI-05 Start from the widget; today's progress on it

**Now.** The widget lists the next pieces; a tap opens the task; the refresh icon re-reads (`widget/NextWidget.kt:198`, `:211`). No action starts work.

**Proposal.** A *Start* button on the first row (an `ActionCallback` that calls `Sessions.start` for the plan's next piece), and a header line "Today: 1 of 4 done, 1 h 20 left". With BL-04, "Finish today → 25 min free" on the same line.

**Caveats.** Glance callbacks run in a broadcast; starting a session there needs the same checks Start in the app does (a session already running, the task gone). The widget's refresh path (`WidgetUpdater`) already handles the plan.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-06 Quick replies under Claude's answers

**Now.** Every turn is typed (`AskActivity.InputBar`, `:462`); the examples are shown only on an empty chat (`EXAMPLES`, `:308`).

**Proposal.** Claude returns up to three `suggestedReplies` (short strings) with each answer, shown as chips above the input ("Yes, move it", "Show tomorrow", "Why?"). A tap sends the chip's text. The model's prompt says the chips must be answers the student might give, not questions.

**Caveats.** Three more short strings in each reply; negligible cost. The chips must never be actions in themselves (an action is a card, tapped on purpose).

**Effort.** S.

## UI-07 Say it instead of typing it

**Now.** Instructions and chat are typed. A sixteen-year-old types short; "I can't do Tuesday because football and the physics is actually due Friday not Thursday" is a sentence more easily said than typed.

**Proposal.** A microphone button on the input bar that starts the system's `RecognizerIntent.ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH` and puts the transcript in the field for the student to check and send. No new permission (the intent runs the system's recogniser); works offline on the Galaxy S24 with Samsung's or Google's engine.

**Caveats.** The transcript goes to the recogniser's provider (Google or Samsung), as any dictation on the phone does. The student edits before sending, so a mishearing can't become an instruction.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-08 "Done today" on the Plan tab

**Now.** The Plan tab shows what's left; what's been done today disappears from it. Stats has the fortnight. Nothing on the main screen says "you've done 1 h 20 of today's 3 h".

**Proposal.** Under today's list, a folded "Done today: 3 pieces, 1 h 20" that opens to the pieces struck through, with their minutes and how they were done (session, photo, source). On a day done in full: "Today's plan is done" and the free time earned.

**Caveats.** The data is in the activity log (sessions, completions); the day record (`Days`) has the planned list. A small read, no new store.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-09 The task sheet in three parts: do, about, change

**Now.** The sheet (`ui/TaskSheet.kt:81`) shows the title, due, kind, steps, and a row of buttons: Done/Edit/Delete for own tasks, "Open in Teams/Gmail/…" for sources, Not a task, Ask Claude about it (`:193–205`). With the additions proposed elsewhere (Claude's reason AI-09, attachments DX-01, why-today PL-07, unsure questions AI-10, "Read again" AC-06, Tomorrow UI-04, Split with Claude AI-19) it would get crowded.

**Proposal.** Three groups with headings: **Do** (Start, Open in source, Check with a photo); **About** (due, with Claude's reason; the steps; attachments; why it's planned where it is; the instructions' text folded); **Change** (Tomorrow, Not a task, Ask Claude, Read again, Edit/Delete). The first group stays above the fold on a phone.

**Caveats.** A rearrangement the student has to relearn; do it once, with the additions, not piecemeal.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-10 The Tasks tab: filters, and the hidden emails

**Now.** Open and finished lists with source tiles above (`ui/TasksScreen.kt:81`, `:103`, `:122`); hidden tasks (Info emails, Not-a-task, waits) are filtered out entirely.

**Proposal.** A chip row to filter by source and by subject (the class map, AI-05), and at the foot a folded "Hidden (12)" section listing what's hidden and why ("only to read, by Claude", "you said not a task", "waits for …"), each with a one-tap way back (AI-11). Search isn't proposed (your v1 call, IMP-P2-010).

**Caveats.** The hidden list shows the emails you chose not to see; folded, it costs nothing until opened.

**Effort.** M. **Decision.** Q-04.

## UI-11 The Calendar page: which calendars, which verdicts

**Now.** The page lists a fortnight's events (`ui/CalendarActivity.kt:211`) and takes the student's answers to the planner's questions.

**Proposal.** A row of calendar chips to include or exclude (DX-13); each event shows its verdict (Free, Busy, "A few hours", Asked) with a tap to change it, so an answer given to a notification can be corrected; the range follows the planner's horizon with a "Further" fold (DX-14); location and description shown when present (DX-12).

**Effort.** S.

## UI-12 The phone's colours, and a large-text check

**Now.** `ui/Theme.kt` uses Material 3's default light and dark schemes; no dynamic colour. 

**Proposal.** `dynamicLightColorScheme`/`dynamicDarkColorScheme` on Android 12+ so the app matches the wallpaper like the phone's own apps. And one pass with the system font at its largest: the DO NOW card and the block screen should still fit without the Start button scrolling off.

**Caveats.** Cosmetic; a teenager may care more than you'd think. The large-text check is the useful half.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-13 Settings that say what they do to the plan

**Now.** Settings is a form of fields with short labels. The effect of a value on the plan ("margin 1 day" means "homework due Thursday is planned for Tuesday") is in the docs.

**Proposal.** Group into sections (Hours, Planning, Blocking, Claude, Sources, Notifications), each field with one line of effect computed from its value: "Weekdays 16:45–22:00: 5 h 15 of planning time a day"; "Margin 1 day: work due Thursday 08:30 is planned by Tuesday"; "3 minutes of work per minute free: a 45-minute session earns 15". A *Preview* at the top of Planning showing today's capacity under the draft values.

**Caveats.** Text that must be kept right when the planner changes; keep the lines computed from the same constants, not typed.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-14 A session view inside the app

**Now.** A running session lives in its notification (a countdown with Stop, `block/Sessions.kt`) and on the DO NOW card. There's no screen that shows the step's text, the time left, and the next step together.

**Proposal.** Tapping the DO NOW card during a session opens a full-screen session view: the step's title and the task's instructions (folded), a large timer, *Help me start* lines (AI-15), *Extend 10 min* and *Done early* (BL-03), and the free time this session earns. It also serves as the "blocked" state's partner: the block screen's Start lands here.

**Caveats.** The timer must follow the uptime clock as `FocusSession` does. One more screen, but it's the one the student looks at most.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-15 Edit a task of your own's steps one by one

**Now.** The own-task form shows "N steps, 1 h 30 in all: its page changes their minutes together" (`ui/OwnTaskForm.kt:160`): steps can be made by Claude when the task is created, not edited after.

**Proposal.** A steps editor in the form: add, remove, rename, set minutes, reorder by drag; the total follows. Ticks are kept by title. Pairs with AI-19 (Claude re-splitting) and the chat's `Steps` change.

**Caveats.** Reusing `withEnrichment`'s title matching keeps ticks; a renamed step loses its tick unless the editor carries the done flag through, which it should.

**Effort.** M.
