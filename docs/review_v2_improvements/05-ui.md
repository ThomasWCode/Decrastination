## UI-01 Time pickers instead of typed times

**Now.** Hours, quiet hours, the briefing time and the check-in time are typed as text ("16:45") and parsed (`ui/SettingsActivity.kt:347`, `parseTime` at `:365`).

**Proposal.** Material 3's `TimePicker` in a dialog from a tap on the field; `Window` fields become two taps with the length shown ("5 h 15").

**Effort.** S.

## UI-02 A load line under each day

**Now.** A day header is a date (`TodayScreen.DayHeader`, `TodayScreen.kt:419`); the list is capped at eight pieces with "and N more".

**Proposal.** Under the date: "2 h 10 planned · 3 h 40 free", a bar in proportion, red when a source deadline can't be met (PL-02). On the Day timeline (UI-17) the free time is visible as empty space, so this line matters most on the week strip and the Work view's grouping by day.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-03 Swipe a piece: done, or later today

**Now.** A piece opens a sheet; the tick is on the sheet or the DO NOW card (`ui/PieceActions.kt`).

**Proposal.** Swipe right: done (tick the step; for an own task's last step, the task; never a source task's "done", which the source says). Swipe left: later today (re-slot after the current slots, PL-13). Each with an undo snackbar. Your answer to Q-17: yes.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-04 Move a piece by hand, to a day or a time, never past its due date

**Now.** "Do it Thursday" goes through Claude or isn't possible.

**Proposal.** Long-press a piece on the Day timeline to drag it to another time (an `AtTime` change) or onto another day on the week strip (an `OnDay` change, PL-04), with no model call; a *Tomorrow* button on the sheet for the common case. No limit on moves (your answer to Q-17); a move that would put the piece after its task's due date, or after the day the spread says is its last usable one, is refused with the reason shown. Moves are instructions, so they're logged and in the chat's context.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-05 The widget: the next slot, a Done tick, today's progress

**Proposal.** The first row is the current or next slot with its time ("17:00–17:45 Statics Prep") and a tick (an `ActionCallback` calling the same tick path as the app); a header line "Today: 1 of 4 done, 1 h 20 left; finish → 25 min free" (BL-04). The micro-plan's first line (AI-15) under the slot when there's room.

**Effort.** M.

## UI-06 Quick replies under Claude's answers

**Now.** Every turn is typed (`AskActivity.InputBar`, `:462`).

**Proposal.** Claude returns up to three `suggestedReplies`, shown as chips above the input; a tap sends the text. Never actions in themselves. The same chips are the quick replies on Felix's messages (AI-22) and notification replies (AI-24).

**Effort.** S.

## UI-07 Say it instead of typing it

**Now.** Instructions and chat are typed.

**Proposal.** A microphone button on the input bar that starts the system's `RecognizerIntent.ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH` and puts the transcript in the field to check and send. No new permission; works offline on the Galaxy S24.

**Caveats.** The transcript goes to the recogniser's provider (Google or Samsung), as any dictation does.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-12 The logo's colours, light and dark

**Now.** `ui/Theme.kt` uses Material You's dynamic colours from the wallpaper on Android 12+, else the default light and dark schemes.

**Proposal.** Your note 5. The logo is a clock on pink with black line art and a white face: `ic_launcher_background` is `#FFAAAA`, the figure's pink reads about `#F8A8A8`, the lines black, the face off-white. A scheme built from it with Material 3's `lightColorScheme`/`darkColorScheme` rather than dynamic colour:

- **Light.** Primary the logo pink (`#FFAAAA`, on-primary near-black `#1A1A1A`); primary container a paler pink (`#FFD6D6`); surface the face's white (`#FCFCFC`); on-surface near-black; secondary a muted rose for the week strip and chips; error the standard red kept for overdue so it stays distinct from the pink.
- **Dark.** Honour the phone's dark theme: surfaces near-black (`#121212`, containers `#1E1E1E`), on-surface off-white; primary the same pink (`#FFAAAA`) as the accent (ticks, the DO NOW card's edge, the chat button, slot bars), on-primary near-black; primary container a deep rose (`#5A2E2E`) for selected states. Pink text on black fails contrast for small type, so pink is for fills and large elements, off-white for body text.
- The widget, the block screen and the notifications' accent (`setColor`) take the same pink. The monochrome launcher icon stays as it is.

**Final state.** The app looks like its icon. Dark mode is dark with pink where the logo is pink.

**Caveats.** Check contrast once with the system font at its largest; the standard red for overdue must stay red (PL-14 makes it the only red on screen). Dynamic colour is dropped; confirmed in Q-35, with the widget, the block screen and the notification accent in pink.

**Effort.** S.

## UI-17 Two screens, not three: Day and Work, with a timeline

**Now.** Three tabs (Plan, Tasks, Stats; `MainActivity.TABS`) and a Calendar page in the menu. Your note 8: the difference between Plan, Tasks and Calendar isn't clear; merge them and make them obvious.

**Proposal.** Two tabs for the work and one for the record:

- **Day.** A timeline of the selected day: the calendar's events as blocks, the slots (PL-12) as cards between them (time, piece, micro-plan's first line, tick, Later), school windows shaded (PL-15), the weekend's *Outside* breaks as their own cards (PL-12), free time as empty space, the "won't fit" line (PL-02) when there is one, "Done today" at the foot (UI-08). A week strip above to pick another day, each day with its load line (UI-02). Today opens by default, scrolled to now. Drag to move (UI-04). This absorbs the Plan tab and the Calendar page: the calendar is the timeline's background, the plan its foreground, and "what do I do next" is the card at the line marked now.
- **Work.** Every open task, grouped by due day by default, by subject on a tap (UI-10): what exists, regardless of when it's planned. Finished and hidden folded at the foot. The place to star-check emails, add your own task, add a photo (NF-01).
- **Stats** stays as a tab; it's the record.

The distinction stated on the screens' first use: *Day is when; Work is what.*

**Final state.** One screen answers "what now and what's today"; one answers "what's on my plate"; nothing is on two.

**Caveats.** The largest UI change here, and it needs PL-12 (slots) to be the timeline's content; before slots exist, Day can be the ordered list with the calendar's events interleaved, which is already better than two screens. Keep `TodayScreen`'s DO NOW card as the timeline's "now" card so nothing you relied on is lost. Confirmed in Q-38: three tabs, today's timeline scrolled to now.

**Effort.** L.
